Background

Operational discipline meets AI systems thinking.

My background in Army aviation and civilian helicopter maintenance shaped how I approach technical work: inspect the real system, respect procedures and authority, preserve evidence, troubleshoot across layers, and do not declare success until the result is verified.

I now apply that discipline to AI-assisted software, Linux diagnostics, workflow design, project recovery, validation systems, networking, virtualization, and internal capability building.
